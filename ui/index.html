Match two lists of names

Umlauts, typos, abbreviations, legal forms, truncation.

Nothing leaves your computer — no upload, no account

How it works

  1. Load two lists. Drop an Excel file or CSV on each side — or select the cells in Excel, copy, and paste them straight into the box.
  2. Match. Names are compared after umlauts, case, punctuation and spacing are folded away, so Müller GmbH, MUELLER GMBH and Mueller G.m.b.H. are all the same name. An abbreviation matches what it stands for: GmbH finds Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung.
  3. Confirm the uncertain ones. Names that are identical after folding are accepted for you. The rest come up one at a time — Y keeps, N discards, 1–9 picks a different candidate. Click any row in the table to go back to it and change your mind.
  4. Export to Excel or CSV when you are done.

Your lists never leave this computer. Turn off your internet — it keeps working.
